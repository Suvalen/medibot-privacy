Privacy Policy

Medi-Bot · Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Medi-Bot (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information. By using Medi-Bot you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Medical disclaimer: Medi-Bot provides general health information generated by artificial intelligence. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider, and in an emergency contact your local emergency services.

1. Who we are

Medi-Bot is operated by Erick Tankawijaya. For any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at erick3005ms@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the app:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register. These are used to create and secure your account.
  • Chat content — the messages you send to the AI assistant and the responses you receive. If you choose to save a chat, that conversation is stored under your account.
  • Authentication data — secure session tokens that keep you logged in. We do not store your password in plain text; authentication is handled by our provider (see below).

We do not collect your location, contacts, device sensors, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app’s core features: account login, AI chat, and saved chats.
  • To respond to your messages by sending them to our AI processing service.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where your data is stored and processed

Account information and saved chats are stored using Supabase, a third-party backend and authentication provider, on their secure cloud infrastructure. Messages you send to the assistant are processed by our AI service to generate a response. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security terms. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

5. Data sharing

We share data only with the service providers necessary to operate the app (such as our backend/authentication provider and AI processing service), and only to the extent needed to deliver the features you use. We may disclose information if required by law. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information and saved chats for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual saved chats at any time within the app. You can also permanently delete your entire account from the app’s Settings screen; this removes your account, profile, and saved chats. Deletion is irreversible.

To request deletion or ask about your data, contact erick3005ms@gmail.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and a managed authentication provider. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

Medi-Bot is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to erick3005ms@gmail.com.
